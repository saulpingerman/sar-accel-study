GitHub releases) are made available under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International License
(CC BY 4.0, https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). Reuse them with attribution to the paper named in

Copernicus Sentinel-2 program keep their own licenses, and the scripts fetch the files from their public sources.
