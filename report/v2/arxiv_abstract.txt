Spotlight SAR image formation was implemented for Google TPUs, an Nvidia GPU and a CPU and measured on three Umbra collections at native size. Factorized backprojection runs in kernels written for each device (Pallas, CUDA, C++); images are scored against a float64 exact backprojection checked against the vendor's image. At float32 accuracy a thousand Panama images cost $0.76 on the L4, $1.62 on a TPU v5e, $1.89 on a v6e and 4.3 times the L4's figure on the CPU. The v6e is fastest, 1.5 times the L4's speed at 3.8 times its hourly price. Float16 on the L4 adds 0.9 dB of error and saves 19%; the TPUs' native single-pass products add 12 dB, mostly as phase error beside bright returns. Polar format is the cheapest algorithm on the L4 but runs 47 to 51 times slower on a TPU, and its image carries an unexplained -32 dB residual.
